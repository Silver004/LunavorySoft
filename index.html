Privacy Policy

Anime Wallpapers: LunavorySoft

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Your wallpaper collection works offline. The app has no account requirement, advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services.

This policy explains how LunavorySoft handles information in the Android app Anime Wallpapers: LunavorySoft (package com.lunavorysoft.animewallpapers) and on this privacy-policy website.

Information the app collects

The app does not collect or transmit personal information to LunavorySoft. It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or personal photo library. The wallpaper collection is included in the app.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the identifiers of wallpapers you mark as favourites in private app storage. Search, filter, and framing choices are used to operate the interface and may be retained temporarily as Android restores a screen. Image-loading components may cache bundled artwork locally. These operations do not send your choices to LunavorySoft.

Setting your wallpaper

When you choose Apply, the app processes your chosen artwork and crop on your device and passes the resulting image to Android's wallpaper service for your home screen, lock screen, or both. The wallpaper permission is used for this purpose.

Sharing, purchases, and Android backups

LunavorySoft does not sell your personal information or share it with advertisers. Google Play handles purchases and distribution under Google's own policies; the app does not collect your payment-card details.

Android backup and device transfer are enabled. Eligible app data, including favourites, may be backed up or transferred by Android or your device provider according to your device settings and their privacy policies. LunavorySoft does not operate or access those backups.

Storage, security, and deletion

Favourites remain in app storage until you remove them or clear the app's storage. Tap a wallpaper's heart to remove it from favourites. To remove local app data, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Android manages access to private app storage.

Backup copies are managed separately through your device or backup provider. An applied wallpaper may remain after uninstalling the app; change it through Android's wallpaper settings.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use that information to answer your request and resolve support issues. Correspondence is retained only as long as needed for those purposes or applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing the address below. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is unnecessary for your question.

Children's privacy

The app does not ask users of any age to provide personal information and has no user accounts or messaging features. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us through support, contact us so we can address it.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or cookies to this page. GitHub may process technical information, including visitors' IP addresses, to provide and secure the hosting service. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting this page is separate from using the offline app.

Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we will update this policy and its date. Relevant changes will also be reflected in the app's privacy policy. Any required disclosures or permissions will be provided before affected features are used.
