Privacy Policy

Japanese Icon Pack · Effective 3 October 2026

Ink & Ember is an offline Android icon pack provided by LunavorySoft. For privacy questions or requests, contact younes.yuusha@gmail.com.

Information handled by the app

The app does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. It has no account system, advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not request access to contacts, photos, location or your installed-app list. Icon searches are processed on your device. Search and screen state may be retained locally by Android to restore your session. You can clear local app data in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Launchers and purchases

Your selected launcher or theme tool reads the artwork and icon mappings supplied by this pack. Its handling of installed apps and icons is governed by its own privacy policy. Google Play handles purchases and may process transaction, device and diagnostic information under Google's policies. Ink & Ember does not receive your payment-card details.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and resolve your request. Email is processed by our email provider. We retain support correspondence only as needed for support, dispute resolution or legal obligations. You can request deletion at younes.yuusha@gmail.com, subject to legal retention requirements. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent personal information through support, contact us to request its removal.

This policy page

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information such as IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not add analytics, advertising or tracking cookies to this page.

Changes

We will update this policy and its effective date if our practices change. Contact: younes.yuusha@gmail.com.
